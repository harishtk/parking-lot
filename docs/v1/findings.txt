2. Spot capacity cannot be exceeded.
3. ACTIVE ticket must correspond to exactly one. 
4. CLOSED ticket cannot be reopened.
5. Every allocation belongs to exactly one spot.
6. Every active parked vehicle must exist in vehicleIndex.
5. Ticket status transition: ACTIVE -> CLOSED
